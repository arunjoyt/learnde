How answers are checked
  • Capital letters, extra spaces and punctuation (. ! ? : ") are ignored.
  • Umlauts and ß must be exact: "uber" does not match "über", "strasse" does not match "Straße".
  • Articles count: "Hund" does not match "der Hund".
  • Text in brackets in the sheet is optional: "laufen" matches "laufen (ist gelaufen)".
  • When a sheet cell has several answers separated by /, ; or , any one of them is correct.
  • A blank answer is wrong.
  • Use the ä ö ü ß buttons if your keyboard does not have these letters.
